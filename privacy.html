Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DevClean ("we", "the site", "the tool") handles information when you use our website.

1. Data You Submit for Processing

DevClean allows you to paste text into the tool to clean spaces, remove duplicate lines, or convert lists to JSON. This text is sent to our server for processing and returned to you immediately. We do not store, log, save to a database, or otherwise retain the text you submit. Once your request is processed, the text exists only in your browser.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site (e.g. number of visits, pages viewed, general location, and referral source). Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this anonymized, aggregated information. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors.

You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data at Google's Privacy Policy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Cookies and Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Third-Party Services

Our site may use third-party services (such as Google AdSense) that collect, monitor, and analyze data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Server Logs

Like most websites, our hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Render) may automatically collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and access times through server logs, for security and operational purposes. We do not use this information to identify individual users.

6. Children's Privacy

DevClean is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the contact information on our GitHub repository.
